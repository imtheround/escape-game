ESCAPE GAME - ART STYLE GUIDELINES
Reference: Soul Knight Aesthetic (e.g., Knight, Wizard)

1. RESOLUTION & GRID
- All characters, mobs, and weapons should fit snugly within a strict 16x16 pixel grid.
- The art must be "chunky" and distinctly retro. Avoid using high-resolution curves or sub-pixels.

2. PROPORTIONS (CHIBI / STUBBY)
- Heads/Helmets/Hoods should be massive, taking up roughly 60% to 70% of the entire character sprite.
- Legs should be extremely short and stubby (usually 1 or 2 pixels high).
- Arms are typically not drawn by default unless the character is actively holding a weapon. The body is effectively just a head sitting on tiny feet.

3. OUTLINES (CRITICAL)
- Every single character, enemy, and weapon MUST have a solid, 1-pixel thick, pure black (#000000) outline tracing its entire outer silhouette. 
- The black outline separates the sprite from the chaotic open-world background and gives it that signature punchy, pop-art look.

4. COLOR PALETTE
- Extremely bright, vibrant, and highly saturated colors. 
- Avoid muddy or washed-out tones. If it's red, it should be a striking, bright red.
- High contrast is key.

5. SHADING & HIGHLIGHTS
- Minimalist, 3-tone cell shading per material (Highlight, Base, Shadow).
- No soft gradients.
- Example: Silver armor should have a solid light silver highlight on the top edge, a base medium grey for the center, and a dark grey shadow near the bottom.

6. FACIAL FEATURES
- Keep faces minimal and heavily stylized.
- Often, characters have their faces obscured by helmets, hoods, or masks.
- Use bright, glowing single-pixel or two-pixel dots for eyes peering out from dark visor slits or shadowy hoods (e.g., glowing red eyes in the dark).
